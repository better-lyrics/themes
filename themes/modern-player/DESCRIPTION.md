A clean and immersive theme for YouTube Music that transforms the default player with edge-to-edge album art, glassmorphic surfaces, a redesigned player bar, and a cinematic background.

## What's New in v2.0
- **Redesigned Player Bar:** Centered playback controls, isolated track info, a unified Like/Dislike pill, and a full-width progress bar.
- **Unified Glass Side Panel:** Rounded translucent container for the queue, lyrics, comments, and related tabs.
- **Distraction-Free Top Bar:** Automatically hides clutter when the player page is open, paired with a pill-shaped Song/Video switcher.
- **Better Lyrics Integration:** Smooth lyric focus blur and refined floating dock spacing.

## Key Features

- **Edge-to-Edge Artwork:** Album covers expand to perfectly fill the player area without awkward black borders.
- **Redesigned Player Bar:** Centered playback controls, truncated track info, a unified Like/Dislike pill, and a full-width progress bar.
- **Unified Glass Side Panel:** Rounded 24px translucent container housing the queue, lyrics, comments, and related tabs.
- **Distraction-Free Top Bar:** Automatically hides search clutter when the player page is open, paired with a pill-shaped Song/Video toggle.
- **Cinematic Lyrics:** Smooth scrolling with depth-of-field blur, opacity effects for inactive lines, and refined floating dock spacing.
- **Immersive Ambient Glow:** The background dynamically adapts to the current song with a high-saturation blur effect.

## Compatibility

- Works with Better Lyrics v2.0.5.6+
